The original renderer, bitmap cache and geometry are retained. The 1,879-line
inner implementation is separated into engine state, gestures, drawing,
geometry, paths and primitives to meet this project's 500-line limit.
Touch taps and hardware keys now call CurlEngine.animateTap and share the
same animation completion callback. The adapter exposes readiness, busy
state and cancellation without injecting pointer events. Layout changes
recreate the engine; focus/lifecycle changes cancel pending navigation.
The dispatchDraw signature is updated for the current Android SDK.

This Android library is independent of the reader app. PTQPageTurnController
exposes readiness, busy state, animated page turns and cancellation. The app
owns volume-key routing and reading/TTS policy, and adapts its navigation to
that public controller. No app classes or resources are used by this module.
